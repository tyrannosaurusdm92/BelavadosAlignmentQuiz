Left Sidebar Command Center

The left sidebar acts as the command center. It contains map navigation, border editing controls, province tools, settlement tools, and import/export controls. On large screens it remains sticky and scrollable so the map can remain visible while editing.

Province Border Mapping System

The border mapping system is the central feature of this build. Every province has a draggable border overlay made from anchor points. The site starts with anchors created from province center, square mileage, and directional-distance data, then lets the DM refine the shape manually.

  • White anchor dots reshape the live province polygon, overlay, generated province image, and settlement coordinate math.
  • Gold province center markers store the official province center latitude/longitude and do not move border anchors unless reset/regeneration is chosen.
  • Anchor and center handles keep the same visible screen size while zooming for precise high-zoom edits.
  • Province maps inherit their coordinate range from the live province geometry instead of fixed legacy image bounds.

Province-Only Settlement Pin System

Settlement pins are only available while editing a province map. They are not placed directly on the main map, keeping province centers and border editing separate from settlement placement.

  1. Open a province from the main map or province selector.
  2. Under population and square mileage, choose Capital City, City, Town, or Village.
  3. Click inside the province map to drop the settlement pin.
  4. The pin auto-generates latitude and longitude for the settlement epicenter.
  5. Drag the pin to reposition it; latitude and longitude auto-correct.
  6. Right-click the pin to delete it.
  7. Select the pin to edit metadata, links, settlement percentages, race categories, gods, danger, transportation, and notes.

The scanner weighs settlement type, race diversity, worship/transport landmarks, tags, danger, and terrain scan results. Settlement population and land scanner

Settlement scanner: available inside a province after every settlement in that province has been saved. It assigns settlement square miles and population from settlement type, tags, race diversity, public transportation, deities, danger, and terrain scan results. Farming villages receive wider land footprints with lower population density; factories, industrial districts, and slums receive denser populations.
