# Dossier implementation matrix

- **Discovery objects and host roles:** Group Finder supports players, groups seeking players, groups seeking a host, hosts offering to run, co-host/safety facilitators, open tables, one-shots, and Right Now openings. Host titles include DM, GM, MOL / Master Of Lore, Keeper, Storyteller, Referee, Facilitator, Host, and custom write-ins.
- **Eligibility before compatibility:** hard role, play-mode, public-anchor, age, and safety boundaries remain before soft matching.
- **Controlled contact:** Group Finder Interests open a pre-game lobby; they do not immediately expose external contact details.
- **Local safety:** in-person posts require a saved public anchor and use 5, 10, 25, or 50 mile radius choices rather than a residential address.
- **Right Now:** marked as expiring and never bypasses age, screening, approval, or public-place requirements.
- **Central safety:** report controls remain available across profiles, posts, messages, members, and groups; reports involving local leadership are described as bypassing implicated local leadership.
- **Free access:** creation, discovery, messaging, system setup, safety, accessibility, and custom systems have no paid tier or boost path.
