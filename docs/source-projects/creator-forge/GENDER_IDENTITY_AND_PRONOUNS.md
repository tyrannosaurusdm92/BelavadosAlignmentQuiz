# Gender Identity and Pronoun Profiles

## Protected pre-approved identities

The built-in registry contains exactly these fourteen protected identity names:

Agender; Bi-Gender; Cis-Female; Cis-Male; Demi-Female; Demi-Male; Gender-Flexible; Gender-Fluid; Gender-Less; Neutrois; Non-Binary; Poly-Gender; Trans-Female; Trans-Male.

Protected means the identity cannot be deleted or renamed. Its aliases, notes, and project-default pronoun forms can be edited and reset.

## Pronoun data

Each profile stores:

- Display label
- Subject
- Object
- Possessive adjective
- Possessive pronoun
- Reflexive
- Singular or plural grammatical agreement

The entity generator may use the profile defaults or a complete per-person override. Individual records remain editable in the JSON record editor after generation.

## Adding identities

Use **People & Entities → Identity and Pronoun Registry → Add Identity**. Custom identity profiles may be edited or deleted. They are saved in the project’s `identityProfiles` array and included in project ZIP exports.

Identity and pronoun selection does not alter body form, ancestry, token art, class, profession, voice, personality, or relationship options.
