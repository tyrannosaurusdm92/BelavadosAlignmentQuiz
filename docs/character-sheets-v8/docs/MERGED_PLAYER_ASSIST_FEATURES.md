# Merged Player Assist Features

- Two full rectangular character-art spots that preserve full-body compositions.
- One separate circular token spot with upload/drop, drag positioning, zoom, horizontal/vertical crop controls, border thickness, color wheel, hexadecimal entry, and uploaded texture/gradient borders.
- Player identity and character identity are tracked separately. Pronouns and gender identities include inclusive preset lists plus write-in fields.
- Relationship and faction managers include status/standing, bond or trust information, favors/debts/heat notes, roleplay prompts, histories, and portal-state persistence.
- Progression and currency popups operate the exact automatic trackers and ledgers used by each sheet, avoiding duplicate or conflicting records.
- Currency dialogs announce gains, spending, conversions, and insufficient funds. Progression dialogs announce awards, readiness, milestones, ranks, advances, improvements, and level-up choices according to the current system.
- System-specific level-up and advancement checklists guide every required choice and recalculation.
- Searchable guidance covers abilities, attacks, spells, moves, powers, feats, Edges, stunts, actions, skills, and other system-specific options. Each detail view explains how to roll or resolve it, how to roleplay it, which modifiers/resources apply, and what the sheet updates afterward.
- Roll controls prepare structured requests for the future parent portal. The character sheets declare `generates_dice: false` and do not add a competing dice engine.
- Global desktop/mobile wrapping and overflow protection covers buttons, labels, controls, rule cards, filenames, generated content, popups, and long unbroken text.
